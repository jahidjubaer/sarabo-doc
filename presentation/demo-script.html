Sarabo — Live Demo Script

Part 1 is the 5-minute demo shown right after the 10 slides. Part 2 is the detailed 12–15 minute version, for rehearsal or extra time. Button names are exactly as they appear on screen.

The story (open the demo with it, 30 seconds)

This is a typical situation, not a real customer.

Problem. Rahim lives in Ambarkhana, Sylhet. His Samsung Galaxy A54 falls and the screen cracks: a green line, and touch stops working. He takes it to a shop. They say "about 2,500 taka", keep the phone for a week, then ask for 4,500. There is no written quote and no receipt. Ten days later the touch fails again. The shop says it is new damage, and Rahim cannot prove otherwise.

Solution with Sarabo. Rahim describes the problem in Banglish on his phone. A verified local technician offers a price before collecting the phone. Rahim pays a 200 taka visit fee. After inspection he sees an itemised quote of 4,000 taka, and the 200 he already paid is deducted. He tracks every step. When the touch fails again, the warranty covers a free re-repair.

Say: "Let me show you exactly that, live."

Rahim's problemWhat Sarabo does
No price up frontCatalogue estimate (1,500–6,000 taka for this repair) shown before anything is booked; technicians send offers with their own estimate
No visibilityA tracking code and a 4-step timeline (Request, Inspect, Approve, Repaired), plus notifications at every step
Surprise billAn itemised quote (labour 800 + parts 3,200 = 4,000) that he approves before any work; the 200 taka visit fee is deducted
No proofThe technician records the phone's condition at pickup; the customer sees it and can dispute it
No warranty30 days: the same technician re-inspects free; a covered fault is re-repaired free or refunded by an admin
Technician isn't paidThe customer pays online before the repair starts; the technician's 90% is held, then released when Rahim confirms he got the phone back

Before the session (do not skip)

  • Use the local app: npm run dev in sarabo-server (port 3000) and in sarabo-client (http://localhost:5173). It has the prepared data.
  • If using the live site (https://sarabo-client-six.vercel.app): set the PC's DNS to 1.1.1.1 or 8.8.8.8 and run ipconfig /flushdns first; this ISP sometimes cannot reach Vercel.
  • Three accounts in three separate browser windows (normal + two private windows, or separate Chrome profiles): Customer, Technician (approved, Sylhet, smartphone skills), Admin.
  • One request already at Quote ready (a full repair takes too long to do live).
  • The finished Samsung Galaxy A54 request with the resolved warranty claim open in a tab.
  • Stripe test card 4242 4242 4242 4242, expiry 12/34, CVC 123.
  • Browser zoom 125%. Close other tabs, turn off notifications, charge the laptop.
  • Optional: a photo of a cracked phone screen on the desktop (for the AI photo check).
  • Backup: the report's Chapter 7 screenshots, in case the internet fails.

Limits to remember: the AI support chat allows 8 questions per 10 minutes for a signed-out visitor, so don't test it repeatedly just before the demo. If Gemini is slow, fill the form by hand; everything works without AI.

Part 2 — Detailed demo (12–15 minutes)

A. Public site (1 minute)

DoSay
Home page"Anyone can see services and prices without an account."
Services"45 services across 8 device types. Every price is in taka and is an estimate; the final price comes after inspection."
Service areas: search Ambarkhana"Rahim's area. The map zooms to the exact neighbourhood, not just the district."
Ask Sarabo: "My phone screen is cracked, how much will it cost?""The AI answer comes from our live catalogue: 1,500 to 6,000 taka, inspection fee 200. It never invents a price."

B. Customer creates the request (1.5 minutes)

DoSay
Create Request → type "Amar Samsung Galaxy A54 phone hat theke pore giye screen fete geche. Display e green line ashe ar touch kaj korche na." → Fill the form for me"Rahim writes in Banglish. Gemini fills in the device, brand, model and the right repair, and writes a clear English description. He can change anything, or press Undo."
Region Sylhet, district Sylhet, address, then a pickup slot"He chooses a 2-hour pickup window. Friday is closed; each slot has limited capacity."
Create request"If no approved technician in Sylhet can do this repair, the request is refused with an explanation instead of waiting forever. Admins see that refused demand, so they know where to recruit."
Optional: Choose photos → Upload"Photos are private. Only Rahim, the admin and the booked technician can see them, through short-lived links."

C. Technician sends an offer (1.5 minutes)

DoSay
Technician window → Find jobs → search Galaxy A54"The technician sees jobs in his region that match his skills. He sees the problem, but not Rahim's name or address."
Send offer"The price guide shows what technicians really offered for this repair recently. The inspection fee has a minimum: 100 taka for small repairs, 200 for bigger ones."
Estimate 3,500–5,000; type "likely display panel and touch, original screen available" → Write it for me → Send application"He types a few words and AI writes a polite note. It removes phone numbers, so contact stays on the platform."

D. Customer chooses and books (1.5 minutes)

DoSay
Customer window → My Requests → open the request"Rahim sees the offers side by side."
Point at the tags"They are ranked by five things Sarabo already knows: rating, reliability, price, district and reply time. The tags say why: 12 repairs, no missed pickups; in your district; usually replies in 2 minutes."
Choose → Pay ৳200 and book → test card → Pay"Paying the visit fee books the technician, all in one database step. If he doesn't come, it's a full refund. If Rahim cancels at least 2 hours before, it's also a full refund."

E. Pickup, inspection and quote (1.5 minutes)

DoSay
Technician → Assigned jobs → Start pickup → Device received; fill the condition"The technician records the phone's condition with Rahim: cracked screen, small scratch on the back, no SIM. This is what Rahim lacked at the shop: proof."
Inspection form: findings, issue, Repairable with parts → Submit inspection"If the phone can't be repaired, either side can close the request. It never gets stuck."
Quote: labour 800, parts 3,200 → Send quote"He sees the 10% commission and what he receives: 3,600."

F. Approve and pay (1 minute)

DoSay
Customer → the request (or the prepared one)"Labour, parts, total, all in writing. Nothing is charged until Rahim approves."
Point at Inspection fee you already paid − ৳200"He pays only what is left: 3,800."
Approve and pay → test card → payment success → Download receipt (PDF)"Stripe's signed webhook is the single source of truth, so a payment is recorded exactly once, even if the page reloads."

G. Repair and hand-over (1 minute)

DoSay
Technician → Start repair → add an update → Mark repair complete"Rahim sees each update."
Mark device returned"If Rahim doesn't answer within 3 days, receipt is confirmed automatically. If he says he hasn't received it, an admin decides."
Customer → Confirm device received → Rate your technician (5 stars)"The technician's money unlocks only now. The review is one per repair and can't be edited or faked."

H. Warranty (1 minute)

DoSay
Customer → the finished Galaxy A54 → Warranty"This is the end of Rahim's story. Ten days later touch failed again."
Show the claim: A replaced part stopped working → re-inspection → Covered → re-repair → Working now"He reported it; the technician had 3 days to book a free visit; he agreed it was the part he fitted and replaced it free. If he hadn't, an admin could take over and refund. Every step has a deadline."

I. Technician wallet and Admin (1.5 minutes)

DoSay
Technician → Wallet → request a withdrawal to bKash"The balance is calculated from records, never stored as a number, so it can't drift."
Admin → Overview"The admin sees what needs action first: missed pickups, requests with no offers, stalled repairs, refunds, disputes."
Withdrawals → Mark paid with a TrxID"The admin sends the money with bKash Send Money, then records the TrxID. Sarabo has no bKash API, so this step is manual, and a TrxID can't be used twice."
Reports → AI summary, then scroll to the charts"Requests against finished repairs, demand by region, and when pickups happen. The AI summary only sees these totals; any sentence with a number that isn't in the real data is removed."
Services & prices → Edit a service"Changing a price creates a new version; requests already made keep their old price."

Close: "So Rahim gets a fair, written price, tracking and a warranty, and the technician gets steady work and guaranteed payment. Thank you."
