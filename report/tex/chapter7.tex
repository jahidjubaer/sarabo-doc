\chapter{AI-Assisted Features}
\label{ch:ai}

Sarabo uses Google Gemini in four places. In each the AI \emph{suggests} and a person \emph{decides}: nothing it returns changes a price, payment or request state without human review. All four are off by default and need an explicit setting and an API key; the client asks \texttt{GET /ai/status} and hides unavailable features. The model is \texttt{gemini-3.5-flash-lite}, with \texttt{gemini-3.1-flash-lite} as fallback when it is slow, overloaded or rate-limited.

\section{AI Customer Support Chat}
A chat on every page answers questions about how Sarabo works, its services and usual prices. The prompt is built from the \emph{live} catalogue (\texttt{supportKnowledgeService}), so prices are always current. History, message and answer length are capped, and follow-up buttons (View services, Track a request, Log in, Create an account) come from a fixed list, not generated links (Figure~\ref{fig:wt-p05}).

\section{AI Request Helper}
On the request form a customer can describe the problem in Bangla, English or mixed ``Banglish'' and/or add a damage photo. The AI drafts the device, catalogue service, brand, model, an English description, a confidence level, an optional follow-up question and, for a photo, whether it shows a device. The form fills itself, the customer edits anything, and \emph{Undo} restores the previous values (Figure~\ref{fig:wt-c02}). A photo showing a device is kept with the request's photos and sent when the customer presses ``Upload'', so it need not be picked twice.

\section{Technician Price Guide and AI Offer Note}
A price guide (computed without AI) shows the usual range from real offers for the same service in the last 180 days: the 25th percentile of ``from'' prices to the 75th percentile of ``to'' prices, and the same for fees; with fewer than three offers it uses the catalogue range. Offers far outside it get an advisory warning.

The technician can ask AI to write the offer note in English or Bangla. The model sees only what the technician sees (device, repair, problem text, whether photos exist), writes in the technician's voice, presents the likely issue as a possibility, says the final price follows inspection, and uses no numbers. Phone numbers (including Bangla digits), emails, links and social handles are stripped, so contact stays on the platform (Figure~\ref{fig:wt-t03}).

\section{AI Summary of Admin Reports}
The Reports page (7, 30 or 90 days) can show an AI summary: a headline, two to four highlights and one to three actions. The model gets only aggregate figures (requests made, finished and cancelled, success rate, average days, refused demand by region, top devices and repairs, workload, inspection-fee money, attention counts), never names, emails or IDs. The last summary per period is stored, so reopening costs nothing (Figure~\ref{fig:wt-a08}).

\section{Safeguards}
Table~\ref{tab:aisafety} summarises how the main risks of LLM features~\cite{owaspllm} are handled.

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\caption{AI risks and safeguards}
\label{tab:aisafety}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}p{3.4cm}X@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Risk} & \textbf{Safeguard in Sarabo} \\
\midrule
Invented catalogue items & The request helper must answer with IDs from an enum built from the live catalogue (JSON schema); the server re-checks them and drops unknown or inactive IDs, or a repair belonging to another device. \\
Invented prices & No AI feature ever sets a price. Estimates come from the catalogue; the offer note contains no numbers. \\
Invented statistics & Every sentence of the report summary is checked: each number must appear in the facts, be a difference or rounded percentage between periods, a small count up to 10, or a fixed context number. Failing sentences are dropped; if nothing remains, the request fails with 502. \\
Prompt injection & Customer text is marked as untrusted data in the prompt, and output is forced into a schema. \\
Personal data leakage & Text containing a card number, password, token or tracking code is refused before any AI call. Models receive no names, emails or addresses. Contact details are stripped from generated notes. \\
Unbounded cost & Per-user 10-minute and daily limits plus a site-wide daily budget per feature, stored in MongoDB so all serverless instances share them. If the database is unreachable, the feature fails closed. \\
Bad photos & JPEG/PNG/WebP only, at most 1.5\,MB, first bytes must match the type; the client shrinks photos to 1024\,px first. The helper's photo is not stored by the AI endpoint. \\
Provider failure & If the first model is slow (over 5\,s for support), overloaded or rate-limited, the request goes straight to the fallback model; otherwise controlled errors (503 unavailable, 504 timeout, 502 invalid answer) and the rest of the form keeps working. Live support answers take 1.4--1.9\,s. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}
